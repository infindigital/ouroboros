(01) How it began

Ouroboros Hospitality was born from the dream of five friends who wanted to create spaces that offer more than accommodation. Spaces that invite you to slow down, find your rhythm and experience the beauty of being present.

We believe true hospitality lies in the little things.

A welcoming smile, a peaceful morning, a thoughtful gesture and the comfort of feeling at home somewhere new. Inspired by the timeless rhythm of nature and the warmth of human connection, that is what we set out to build.

Our spaces are designed to make the most of their surroundings while offering the comfort and ease of a considered stay. Thoughtful rooms, warm interiors, restful beds, clean and well appointed bathrooms, reliable connectivity and inviting common areas come together to create a stay that feels effortless. From quiet corners to take in the views to spaces where conversations unfold over a meal or a cup of tea, every detail is meant to help you settle into the rhythm of the mountains.

(02) The setting

Where mountains, river and sky meet.

Located just beyond the Atal Tunnel, Koksar is where the landscape opens into a dramatic meeting of mountains, river and sky. With the Pir Panjal range rising behind us and the great Western Himalayas unfolding ahead, the property offers expansive 360 degree views in every direction.

The Pir Panjal

The great range rises right behind us, keeping watch over the valley through every season.

The Western Himalayas

Unfolding ahead of you, peak after peak, with light that changes by the hour.

The Chandra River

Flowing through the valley nearby, adding a constant sense of movement and calm.

Koksar is more than a stop on the way to the mountains.

It is a place to pause, breathe and experience the raw beauty of Lahaul. Here, changing light moves across the peaks, the river carries the sound of the valley, and every season reveals a different character of the Himalayas.

Whether you arrive after crossing the Atal Tunnel, are exploring the surrounding valleys, or simply looking for a quiet escape, the setting invites you to stay a little longer.

(03) The name

A circle that never ends.

Ouroboros symbolises a continuous cycle. An idea of renewal, connection and journeys that come full circle.

It reflects what we hope every stay becomes: an experience that stays with you long after the journey ends. Everything returns, and we hope you do too.
