(01) Things to do near Ouroboros

Your Lahaul bucket list.

Hover or tap a card to read more. Ask us and we will help you plan the day.

01Right here

Chandra River sightseeing

The Chandra flows through the valley right by the property. Walk its banks, listen to the water and watch the light change on the slopes.

02Walkable

Explore Koksar Village

Wander through the village, meet the locals and see everyday life in one of Lahaul's first villages beyond the tunnel.

03Seasonal

Snow play & snowman building

When the snow arrives, the whole valley turns into a playground. Snowball fights strongly encouraged.

04~12 km

Scenic drive through Atal Tunnel

One of the most iconic drives in the Himalayas, with the landscape opening up dramatically as you exit into Lahaul.

05~14 km

Visit Sissu Lake

A calm high altitude lake framed by peaks. Bring a flask of chai and stay a while.

06~14 km

Visit Sissu Waterfall

Watch the water tumble down the cliffs, one of the most photographed spots in the valley.

07Anytime

Sightseeing & photography

360 degree views mean every direction is a frame. Peaks, clouds, river and sky, all from the property.

08Daily

Sunrise & sunset viewing

Watch the peaks blush pink at dawn and glow gold at dusk. Best enjoyed with someone special.

09Easy

Local village walks

Gentle walks along fields, stone houses and mountain paths. Slow travel at its best.

10Day trip

Monasteries & sacred sites

Visit local monasteries and religious sites and feel the deep calm of Lahaul's spiritual heritage.

11At Ouroboros

Authentic Himachali cuisine

Traditional flavours, freshly prepared on request in our kitchen.
