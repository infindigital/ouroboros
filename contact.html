(04) Good to know

Quick answers.

How do I book a room?

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 78766 55220, or send the enquiry form above. We will confirm availability and rates directly with you.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is optional and available on request at an additional charge. Authentic Himachali meals can also be freshly prepared on request.

How do I get to Koksar?

Ouroboros is just 2.2 km from the north portal of the Atal Tunnel. Most guests drive up from Manali through the tunnel. On-site parking is available for guests travelling by road.

Are the rooms warm in winter?

Yes. Every room has room heating, and there is a geyser with hot water so you stay comfortable through chilly mountain nights.

Are activities available all year?

Activities are seasonal and subject to weather, road conditions, local availability and applicable charges. Ask us before your trip and we will tell you what is open.
